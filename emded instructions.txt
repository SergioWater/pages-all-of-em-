Steps (high level):

Convert Miguel's Instagram to a Business or Creator account (free, takes 2 min in Instagram settings)

Connect it to a Facebook Page (also required, also free)

Create a Meta Developer account at developers.facebook.com

Create a new app, add the "Instagram Graph API" product

Generate a long-lived access token (60 days, then you refresh it)

Write JS or PHP that calls https://graph.facebook.com/v18.0/{user-id}/media?access_token=...

Parse the JSON response and render <img> tags with your existing HBK styling
