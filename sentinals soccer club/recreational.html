Recreational Program

Fun, fundamentals, and friends. Soccer for every kid.

Sign up for RECREATIONAL SOCCER

Fall 2026 Season

Our recreational program is a 10-week season designed for players who want to learn the game, have fun, and stay active. Two practices per week plus weekend games give your child consistent development in a positive environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

When are the seasons? Fall (August–November) and Spring (February–May). 10 weeks each.
What equipment does my child need? Shin guards, cleats, and a water bottle. We provide the uniform kit and game balls.
How long are practices and games? Practices are 60 minutes, twice per week. Games are 40–60 minutes on weekends depending on age group.
What will my child learn? Ball control, dribbling, passing, shooting basics, teamwork, sportsmanship, and game awareness. Our coaching staff creates fun, age-appropriate sessions that develop skills progressively.
