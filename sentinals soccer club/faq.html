Sentinels Soccer Club — Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions Morgan Hill, Gilroy, and Hollister families ask most.

What ages and cities does Sentinels Soccer Club serve? Sentinels Soccer Club serves boys and girls ages 5 to 18 in Morgan Hill, Gilroy, Hollister, and the surrounding South County, California area. The club has trained youth players in the region since 2015.
What programs does the club offer? The club runs a year-round competitive program (ages 5 to 18) in NorCal Premier Soccer leagues and tournaments, a recreational program with 10-week Fall and Spring seasons, week-long summer day camps for ages 5 to 12, and private one-on-one or small-group training.
When are recreational seasons? Recreational soccer runs two 10-week seasons: Fall (August through November) and Spring (February through May). Each season includes two practices per week plus weekend games.
What equipment does my child need? For recreational play, your child needs shin guards, cleats, and a water bottle. The club provides the uniform kit and game balls. For tryouts, bring shin guards, a water bottle, and a completed registration form.
How long are practices and games? Recreational practices are 60 minutes, twice per week. Games are 40 to 60 minutes on weekends, depending on the age group.
How do competitive tryouts work? May 2026 competitive tryouts are complete and rosters are set. The next open tryouts are planned for 2027 for boys and girls birth years 2009 through 2019 (no club experience required). Sessions are typically held at Christmas Hill Park in Gilroy and the Morgan Hill Adult School. Contact the club to join the 2027 interest list, or register for recreational league now — no tryout required.
How much do summer camps cost? Summer day camps are $150 per week for ages 5 to 12, running 9 AM to 12 PM at the Morgan Hill Community Adult School. Camps include station work, dribbling and ball work, technical drills, goal-scoring drills, and scrimmages.
Is private training available? Yes. One-on-one and small-group private training is available with Coach Nolan and Coach Rick. Coach Nolan brings over 15 years of coaching experience. Sessions focus on individual skill development, position-specific work, and game preparation.
How do I register or get in touch? You can email sentinelssoccerclub@gmail.com or call (408) 722-1028. Recreational sign-ups and account login are handled through the club's registration portal.
