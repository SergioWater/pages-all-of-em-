Sentinels Soccer Club Competitive Soccer Program

Competitive Program

Year-round training for boys and girls, ages 5 to 18. Structured practices, league play, and tournaments.

Our competitive program builds complete players. Athletes develop technical, tactical, physical, and mental skills through structured training and high-level match play. Teams compete in NorCal Premier Soccer leagues and select tournaments throughout the year.

Boys' Competitive

Teams from U6 through U19. Year-round training with fall and spring league seasons, plus tournament opportunities. Players develop in a competitive environment designed to challenge and improve every aspect of their game.

Multiple training sessions per week with experienced coaching staff. Age-appropriate development pathways ensure players advance at the right pace.

Girls' Competitive

Full competitive pathway for female athletes, U6 through U19. Same high-quality coaching and training structure as our boys' program. Girls compete in NorCal Premier Soccer leagues and regional tournaments.

We are committed to growing girls' soccer in South County and providing every female player with a pathway to excel.

Proven Winners

Our competitive teams don't just train — they win. Sentinels squads have earned State Cup titles, NorCal Premier podium finishes, Best of the Best honors, and futsal championships across both our boys' and girls' programs.

Private Training Available

Looking for one-on-one or small-group coaching outside team practice? Our Private Training program offers personalized sessions with Coach Rick, Coach Nolan, and Coach Alex — focused on individual skill development, position-specific work, and game preparation.
