About Sentinels Soccer Club

About Us

A Morgan Hill, Gilroy, and Hollister club built on hard work, respect, and players who keep getting better.

Who We Are

Sentinels Soccer Club has trained youth players in Morgan Hill, Gilroy, Hollister, and the surrounding South County since 2015. We run competitive teams, recreational leagues, and summer camps for boys and girls ages 5 to 18.

Your kid shows up. Our coaches get to work. They leave a better player and a better teammate.

Our Mission

We develop young athletes through structured training, competitive play, and a club culture built on respect and hard work. Soccer teaches confidence, discipline, and how to rely on other people. Our coaches reinforce those lessons at practice, on game day, and between seasons.

What Sets Us Apart

A Spot for Every Player

Five-year-olds trying soccer for the first time train alongside teenagers chasing college rosters. We match players to the right program and the right level of competition.

Year-Round Training

Our competitive program runs all year with short breaks at the holidays and after school ends. Recreational and summer camp programs fill the gaps. Your player stays sharp in every season.

Coaches Who Build Complete Players

Our staff teaches dribbling, passing, shooting, and positioning. They also teach fitness, mental toughness, and game awareness. Technical skill without tactical thinking wins nothing.

Families Across Three Cities

Sentinels families come from Morgan Hill, Gilroy, Hollister, and the surrounding towns. You will see the same parents on the sidelines week after week. That is how a club becomes a community.

Our Values

Compete Beyond the Field

Character, resilience, and sportsmanship carry into school, work, and life.

Embrace Diversity

We welcome players and families of all backgrounds.

Grow From Feedback

Players learn to listen, adapt, and improve.

Fuel Your Ambition

Set goals. Put in the work. Hit them.

Live a Healthy Lifestyle

Physical fitness and positive habits start on the pitch.

Our Programs

Competitive Soccer

Year-round training for ages 5 to 18. Structured practices, league play, and tournaments. Players develop technical, tactical, and mental skills at a high level.

View Program →

Recreational Soccer

A 10-week season (Fall and Spring) with two practices per week and weekend games. Players learn the fundamentals in a positive setting. Nike uniform kit included.

View Program →

Summer Camps

Week-long day camps for ages 5 to 12. Station work, dribbling drills, scrimmages, and goal-scoring challenges. Players build skills and stay active between seasons.

View Program →

Serving the South County Community

Sentinels started in Morgan Hill in 2015 and has grown to serve families across Gilroy and Hollister. Whatever program your child joins — competitive, recreational, private training, or summer camps — they'll train with coaches who understand what South County youth soccer looks like and what it takes to develop players who can compete at the next level.
