Sentinels Soccer Club Summer Soccer Camps — Morgan Hill, CA

Summer Camps

Week-long day camps to keep your player active and improving all summer.

Ages
5 – 12
Time
9 AM – 12 PM
Cost
$150 / week

Our summer camps give young players a fun, structured soccer experience during school break. Each week-long camp focuses on building fundamental skills through engaging drills, small-sided games, and scrimmages.

What Campers Work On

Station Work Dribbling / Ball Work Technical Drills Goal Scoring Drills Scrimmaging

Camps are led by Sentinels coaching staff who bring energy, patience, and expertise to every session. Players of all skill levels are welcome — from beginners to experienced club players looking to sharpen their game.

Location

Morgan Hill Community Adult School
Morgan Hill, California
