Common Gilroy tree-care needs

Larger and rural lots

Acreage and ranchette properties often need brush removal, fuel reduction, and clearing in addition to tree work — with equipment access to match.

Dry-season fuel management

Grass and brush that green up in winter cure into fuel by summer, making weed abatement and defensible-space work a recurring priority.

Mature shade and yard trees

In-town homes still have established trees that need structural pruning, clearance, and the occasional removal.
