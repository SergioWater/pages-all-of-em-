Palm Care

Palm Tree Services

Palms aren't pruned like broadleaf trees, and over-trimming them does real harm. We care for palms the right way — clean, safe, and species-appropriate.

Bay Area palms bring a distinctive look to homes and streetscapes, but they have specific needs. Over-pruned "hurricane cut" palms are stressed and weakened; neglected palms drop heavy fronds and fruit and can harbor pests.

We handle trimming, removal, and ongoing maintenance with palm-specific technique and safety.

Our palm services

Palm trimming

Removing dead fronds, seed pods, and fruit — without over-cutting healthy green fronds.

Palm removal

Safe removal of unwanted, dead, or hazardous palms, including tall specimens.

Palm maintenance

Scheduled care to keep palms clean, healthy, and free of hazards year-round.

The right way to prune a palm

Green fronds feed the palm. Removing too many — the over-thinned "hurricane cut" — stresses the tree and slows growth. We take dead and dying fronds and seed pods, and keep the healthy canopy the palm needs.
