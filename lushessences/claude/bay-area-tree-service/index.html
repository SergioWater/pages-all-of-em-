What we do

Full-service tree care

From a single hazard tree to a property-wide maintenance program, we cover the work Bay Area trees need.

Tree Removal

When a tree is dead, declining, structurally unsafe, or simply in the wrong place, removal is sometimes the responsible choice. Our crews plan every removal around your property, access, and the trees you want to keep.

Learn more

Tree Pruning & Trimming

Good pruning is the single best investment in a tree's long-term health and safety. Bad pruning — topping, lion-tailing, over-thinning — does lasting damage. We prune to industry standards, not shortcuts.

Learn more

Emergency Tree Service

A failed limb over a roof, a tree down across a driveway, a hazardous lean after a storm — these need prompt, careful attention. Here's how to handle the situation and how we help.

Learn more

Commercial Tree Services

Property managers, HOAs, and facility teams need tree care that's scheduled, documented, and low-drama. We build maintenance programs around your site, tenants, and budget.

Learn more

Land Clearing

Overgrown lots, dense brush, and unmanaged vegetation create fire fuel, hide hazards, and block use of your land. We clear responsibly — keeping the trees worth keeping.

Learn more

Consulting Arborist

Sometimes you need answers, not just cuts: Is this tree safe? Will it survive the remodel? What does the city require? A consulting arborist gives you an independent, documented assessment.

Learn more

Palm Tree Services

Palms aren't pruned like broadleaf trees, and over-trimming them does real harm. We care for palms the right way — clean, safe, and species-appropriate.

Learn more
