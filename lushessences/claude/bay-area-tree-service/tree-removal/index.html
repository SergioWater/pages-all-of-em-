Tree Removal

Bay Area Tree Removal, Done Safely

When a tree is dead, declining, structurally unsafe, or simply in the wrong place, removal is sometimes the responsible choice. Our crews plan every removal around your property, access, and the trees you want to keep.

Removing a mature tree is rarely as simple as "cut and drop." On Bay Area lots — many with fences, pools, power drops, and neighbors just a few feet away — the work is a rigging problem as much as a cutting one. We assess the lean, decay, targets, and access before a single cut is made.

Below you'll find how we approach removals, when removal is (and is not) the right call, and the specialized options we offer for large or hard-to-reach trees.

When removal is the right call

  • The tree is dead, or more than roughly half its canopy has died back
  • Structural defects — major cracks, cavities, or root-plate lifting — make failure likely
  • The tree is too close to a foundation, pool, or structure to manage long-term
  • A species is chronically diseased or invasive for the site
  • Construction or hardscape plans leave no viable root zone
We won't oversell removal. If pruning, cabling, or a risk assessment is the smarter option, we'll tell you. Many "remove it" trees can be kept safely for years.

How a professional removal works

Assessment & plan

We evaluate the tree, targets, and access, then choose a drop, rigging, or crane approach and confirm any permit steps with you.

Site protection

Lawns, beds, driveways, and nearby plants are protected. Drop zones and rigging points are set before climbing.

Controlled dismantle

Sections are lowered under control — no uncontrolled drops near structures — using climbing, aerial lift, or crane as the site requires.

Cleanup & haul-off

Wood, brush, and debris are chipped or hauled. We can leave firewood rounds or mulch on request, and grind the stump as a linked service.
