Programs

Judo classes every day for kids, teens and adults — plus yoga, 7 days a week.

Judo programs

We run Judo classes every day for kids, teens and adults. Classes are age-based: Youth, Junior, and Adult. New students are placed by age, and coaches may recommend adjustments based on focus, safety, and readiness.

Youth Judo

Judo promotes physical fitness, mental discipline, and social skills while emphasizing safe practices. Our dojo's quality equipment and expert guidance create a secure environment for all practitioners.

See the schedule

Junior Judo

Training emphasizes throws, pins, and grappling, helping students develop coordination, confidence, discipline, and problem-solving skills in a safe, structured environment.

See the schedule

Adult Judo

Judo is scalable to all ages and fitness levels. Classes emphasize safety, progression, and learning at your own pace. Many adults start with no prior athletic background.

See the schedule

Not sure which class is right for your child? New students are placed by age, and coaches may recommend adjustments based on focus, safety, and readiness. Ask us.

Yoga at Kensei

We offer a variety of yoga styles to suit your needs: Hatha, Vinyasa, Restorative and Ashtanga.

Classes are held 7 days a week, 9:30am to 10:30am at the Kensei Judo dojo in the morning before the martial arts classes later in the day.
