Welcome to HBK

Humble Ballers Klub

HBK Futsal is more than a team — we're a community built around skill, discipline, and the love of the game. From our men's and women's NFPL squads to our youth pathway, every player wears the badge with pride.

Based in San Jose, California, we develop athletes who play the game the right way: technically sharp, mentally tough, and always humble.

The People Behind HBK

Coaches & Staff

Decades of combined futsal experience leading the men's and women's NFPL programs, the youth pathway, and everything in between.

Founder & Owner

Miguel Medina

As Founder and Owner of HBK Futsal, Miguel leads the club's vision, growth, and culture — day-to-day operations, recruitment, coaching structure, sponsorships, and media.

Manager

Miguel Medina Reyes

Manages logistics, scheduling, and travel for the men's and women's squads. Keeps the operation running so coaches and players can focus on the game.

Technical Director & Men's Head Coach

Víctor Miramontes

Newly announced Technical Director. Leads the men's NFPL squad and shapes the tactical identity of the whole club — possession, pressing, and ruthless transitions.

Women's Head Coach

Jeffrey Serrano

Leads the women's NFPL squad. Focuses on technical development and competitive mentality from session one.

Women's Team Manager

Sofía Rentería

Runs day-to-day operations for the women's program — communication, travel, and player support.

Youth Boys Coach

Hyago Galdino

Leads the youth boys pathway. Develops the next generation of HBK players with a focus on fundamentals and game IQ.

Strength & Conditioning

Andrés Fierro

Designs the strength and conditioning program — keeps players fit, fast, and durable across the long NFPL season.
