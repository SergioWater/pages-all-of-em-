Private Training

One-on-one and small-group sessions designed around the individual player.

Personal Coaching. Real Results.

Every player has a next step. Private training is how you get there faster — focused reps, direct feedback, and a plan built around what your player needs most.

One-on-one and small-group sessions are available with Coach Nolan, Coach Rick, and Coach Alex. Coach Nolan brings over 15 years of coaching experience. Private training focuses on individual skill development, position-specific work, and match preparation — the details that team practice does not always have time for.

Who Private Training Is For

Private training works for players at every level. It is not just for the top competitive athletes — it is for any player who wants to grow. Common reasons families choose private sessions:

  • Newer players who need extra reps to build confidence with the ball.
  • Rec league players ready to level up before trying out for competitive.
  • Competitive team players who want to sharpen a specific weakness or position skill.
  • Goalkeepers looking for position-specific training you cannot get in a team session.
  • High school players preparing for tryouts, showcases, or the next season.
  • Returning players coming back from a break or an injury who need to rebuild rhythm.

What Sessions Cover

Ball Mastery

First touch, close control, and confidence with both feet under pressure.

Shooting & Finishing

Technique, placement, and decision-making in the final third.

Passing & Vision

Weight of pass, timing, and scanning the field before you receive.

Defending

Positioning, body shape, and reading the game to win the ball back.

Goalkeeper Training

Distribution, footwork, positioning, and shot-stopping fundamentals.

Game Awareness

Reading play, tactical decisions, and playing your position at a higher level.

Fitness & Speed

Sport-specific conditioning that translates directly to match performance.

Tryout Prep

Targeted sessions leading up to competitive tryouts or high school selections.

How It Works

One-on-One Sessions

A single player and a coach. Fully personalized to that player's goals, weaknesses, and current level. This is the fastest way to see focused improvement.

Sessions are typically 45 to 60 minutes and can be scheduled weekly, biweekly, or in block series leading up to a specific goal (tryouts, showcase, high school season).

Small-Group Sessions

Two to four players training together. Great for teammates who want to grow together, siblings, or friends at a similar skill level. Adds competitive reps and game-realistic pressure while keeping the coaching personal.

Small-group sessions are more affordable per player and often produce faster tactical growth thanks to the live opponent element.
