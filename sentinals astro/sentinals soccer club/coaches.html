Sentinels Soccer Club Coaching Staff — Morgan Hill, Gilroy, and Hollister

Our Coaches

Collegiate athletes and experienced trainers who put player development first.

The Sentinels coaching staff combines college and semi-pro playing experience with a shared commitment to youth development. Our Director of Coaching holds USSF and NSCAA licenses; our staff coaches include former collegiate athletes who understand what it takes to grow as a player and as a person. We coach the whole player.

Our Coaching Philosophy

We believe soccer is a vehicle for growth. Every child who joins Sentinels is met where they are and challenged to become better than they were yesterday. Our coaches are here to nurture confidence, sharpen skill, and build the character that carries into school, family, and every part of life after the whistle.

Development is not one-size-fits-all. A five-year-old learning to trap a ball, a twelve-year-old fighting for a starting spot, and a high schooler chasing a college roster each need a different kind of coaching — but all of them deserve the same care, patience, and belief. That is the Sentinels standard, and every coach on our staff holds themselves to it.

We teach the technical foundations — dribbling, passing, shooting, defending, positioning. We also teach the harder lessons: how to lose, how to lead, how to keep working when it is not easy, and how to lift teammates up on the days they need it most. Those are the skills that outlast any season.

Meet the Staff

Coach Rick (Ricardo Guzman)

Director of Coaching & Technical Director

Coach Rick leads the Sentinels coaching program as Director of Coaching and Technical Director. He shapes the training curriculum across all age groups and works directly with players in both team sessions and one-on-one development.

He played for Soquel High School Varsity, Cabrillo College, and semi-pro for the Bay Area Major League and Santa Cruz Surf. His coaching background includes Soquel High JV and Varsity, Santa Cruz Soccer Camp, SC Scorpions Class 1, and Gilroy Soccer League. Credentials include a USSF Level F license and an NSCAA Level 4 license.

Contact: coachrickfutbol@gmail.com

Coach Nolan (Nolan Grieve)

Collegiate Athlete · Coach · USSF C License

Coach Nolan Grieve is a former collegiate soccer athlete and a college graduate. He brings the discipline, tactical awareness, and match-level intensity of the college game to every Sentinels training session, drawing on over 15 years of coaching experience. He holds a U.S. Soccer C license.

Nolan works with players across our competitive program and leads private training sessions. Players and parents describe him as patient, technical, and quick to celebrate the small breakthroughs that add up to real growth.

Coach Alex (Alexandria)

Collegiate Athlete · Coach

Coach Alex (Alexandria) is a former collegiate soccer athlete and a college graduate. She trains players with the same standards that shaped her own game at the college level — high work rate, clean technique, and constant learning.

Alexandria coaches across age groups and specializes in position-specific development. Her approach is built on connection first — knowing each player, understanding what motivates them, and pushing them to reach it.
