Our Programs

Competitive teams, recreational leagues, private training, and summer camps for boys and girls ages 5 to 18.

Recreational Soccer

Fall 2026 registration is open — the season starts in August. A 10-week season with two practices per week and weekend games. Players learn the fundamentals in a positive setting.

Sign Up → Program details →

Competitive Soccer

Year-round training for ages 5 to 18. Structured practices, league play, and tournaments. Players develop technical, tactical, and mental skills at a high level.

View Program →

Private Training

One-on-one and small-group sessions built around your player's goals. Focused work on finishing, first touch, and decision making with a Sentinels coach.

View Program →

Summer Camps

Week-long day camps for ages 5 to 12. Station work, dribbling drills, scrimmages, and goal-scoring challenges. Players build skills and stay active between seasons.
